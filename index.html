Nkabom

Save Together, Collect in turns.

Nkabom helps susu and savings groups keep clear records: who has paid this month, who collects next, and when each payout happened. Everyone in the group sees the same schedule on their own phone.

Get it on Google Play

How it works

  1. The organiser creates a group, sets the monthly amount and adds members by phone number.
  2. Members install Nkabom and sign in with that same number. The group appears automatically.
  3. Each month the organiser marks who has paid and confirms the payout. Everyone sees it live.

Nkabom keeps records only. It does not collect, hold or send money.
