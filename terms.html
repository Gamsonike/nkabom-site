1. What Nkabom is, and isn't

Nkabom is a record-keeping tool for rotating savings groups (susu). It helps a group keep track of who has contributed and whose turn it is to collect.

Nkabom does not collect, hold, transfer or guarantee any money. It is not a bank, a savings scheme, a lender or a payment service. All money moves directly between group members, outside the app, under the group's own arrangement.
