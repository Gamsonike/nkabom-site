Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Nkabom app ("Nkabom", "we", "us") collects, why, and the choices you have. Nkabom is provided by [YOUR NAME], Ghana. If you have any questions, contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL].

In short: we collect only what's needed to run your savings group: your phone number, your name, and the group records you and your organiser create. We don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and Nkabom never collects, holds or sends money.

1. Information we collect

InformationWhy we need it
Phone numberTo sign you in with a one-time SMS code, and to show you the groups you have been added to.
Your nameSo your group can see who you are.
Group records: group name, monthly amount, members' names and phone numbers, payout order, who has paid each month, and payout datesThis is the main purpose of the app. The organiser enters these records, and everyone in the group can see them.
Subscription statusTo unlock larger groups. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your card or Mobile Money details.
Technical information: device type, operating system version, app version, and diagnostic logs from our service providersTo keep the app secure and working.

Reminders (for example "your contribution is due in 3 days") are scheduled on your own phone. Your reminder settings stay on your device.

2. Information about other people

When an organiser adds members, they enter those people's names and phone numbers. Organisers should only add people who have agreed to join the group. If you were added to a group and don't want to be, ask the organiser to remove you or contact us.

3. Who can see your information

  • Your group: the organiser and every member of a group can see that group's records, including members' names and phone numbers.
  • Our service providers: we use Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to sign you in and store group records. Your information is stored on Google's servers (region: [FIRESTORE LOCATION, e.g. europe-west1]). Google processes it on our behalf under its own security and privacy terms.
  • App stores: Google Play and the Apple App Store process subscription purchases.
  • The law: we may disclose information if the law requires it.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. How we protect it

Information is encrypted in transit. Access to group records is restricted by server-side security rules, so only people in a group can read it, and only the organiser can change it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, and every group you organise, within 30 days. Records you appear in as a member of someone else's group stay with that organiser's group, because they form part of the group's shared history. Ask the organiser, or contact us, to have them removed.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: you can see your information in the app. Ask your organiser to correct group records, or contact us.
  • Deletion: delete your account at any time in the app (account menu → Delete my account), or see how to delete your account.
  • Notifications: turn reminders off in the app or in your phone's settings.

You may also have rights under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), including the right to complain to the Data Protection Commission.

7. Children

Nkabom is not intended for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, let you know in the app.
